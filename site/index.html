How to install
  1. Open the link on your Android phone. If the browser warns about the file type, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open wellbeing.apk. When Android asks, allow installs from this source, then tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details, then Install anyway.

A newer version installs over the old one and keeps what you've saved.

Privacy

Your check-ins, sketches and notes are saved on your device. With an account they're also backed up privately, so you can use them on another device, and other people using the app can't see them. Feedback you send and anonymous likes on exercises help us improve them.
