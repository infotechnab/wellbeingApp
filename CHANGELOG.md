# What's new

Each version's changes, newest first. The app's home page shows this list, so write it for the
people using the app. Add a section whenever `version` in `app.json` goes up.

## 1.3.2

- The app has a new ID, `com.digitalaya.wellbeing`, ready for TestFlight and the app stores.
  On Android, this version installs as a new app next to the old one: delete the old Wellbeing
  app after you install it. If you haven't made an account, check-ins, sketches and notes from the
  old app don't move across.

## 1.3.1

- A new logo: eight soft petals like the feelings wheel, with one filled in for the feeling you
  pick. You'll see it as the app icon, on the opening screen and on the website.

## 1.3.0

- New Notifications section in Profile with a Morning (8 am), Midday (1 pm) and Evening (8 pm)
  check-in. The morning one is on to start with, and you can switch any of them on or off.
- The app asks once whether it may send notifications, the first time you reach the feelings wheel.

## 1.2.0

- Daily reminder: turn it on in Profile and pick a time. Once a day a notification asks how you're
  feeling, and you can tap a feeling right on it to check in without opening the app. It offers the
  two feelings you pick most, plus "Other feeling" to open the full wheel. On iPhone, press and hold
  the notification to see the buttons.
- Check-ins from the reminder show in the Journal as quick check-ins.

## 1.1.0

- Send feedback from Profile: write up to 200 words, record a voice message of up to 5 minutes, or
  both. Only the Wellbeing team reads and listens to it.
- Feedback after an exercise can also be up to 200 words.

## 1.0.1

- Your check-ins, sketches and notes now stay on your phone until you create an account. Likes and
  feedback still reach us, so we can keep improving the exercises.

## 1.0.0

The first test release.

- Check in with how you feel on the emotion wheel, then try a few short, body-based exercises picked
  for that feeling, each with an animated guide.
- Draw how you feel in a sketch, and find your check-ins, sketches and notes in the Journal.
- Use everything without an account, or create one to back up your journal and use it on another
  device.
- "Need help now?" lists Nepal's emergency numbers and the free 24-hour helpline, 1166.
- Rate each exercise with a thumbs up or down, or send feedback in your own words.
- Profile shows the version you have.
